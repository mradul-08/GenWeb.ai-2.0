About Us

At Spice Route we bring the vibrant flavors of Kerala’s coast to Kochi. Our chefs use locally sourced seafood, traditional spices, and time‑honored cooking techniques to create dishes that feel like a seaside celebration. Whether you’re a food enthusiast or a local diner, you’ll find a warm, inviting atmosphere and a menu that tells a story of the sea.
